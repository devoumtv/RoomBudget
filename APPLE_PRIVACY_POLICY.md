# Privacy Policy for Room Budget on the Apple App Store

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026  
**App name:** Room Budget  
**Bundle ID:** com.ldhkuqw.roombudgetfv0t  
**Store:** Apple App Store  
**Platform:** iPhone and iPad

This policy explains how the iOS app Room Budget handles information. It is the privacy policy for the Apple App Store listing.

## Summary

Room Budget estimates furniture costs on your iPhone or iPad. It does not require an Apple ID inside the app, and it does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The lists you create stay on your device.

## Information the app does not collect

Room Budget does not collect:

• Name, email address, phone number, or Apple ID
• Location
• Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
• Advertising identifiers or the device’s IDFA
• Device identifiers used for tracking
• Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics sent to the developer
• Payment or purchase information

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or social-network services. It does not ask you to sign in.

## Information stored on your device

When you use the app, it saves what you type so the list is still there the next time you open it:

• Room names and emoji
• Furniture names and emoji
• Prices and quantities

This information is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to the developer and it is not stored on a server. If you type a person’s name into a room or item name, that text remains on your device. The developer never receives it.

## Clipboard

The Total page has a Copy estimate button. When you tap it, Room Budget places a text copy of your rooms, items, and totals on the iOS clipboard. The app does not read other clipboard contents. It does not send the copied text to the developer or to any other service. Pasting that text into Notes, Mail, or another app is controlled by you.

## How information is used

The app uses the information you enter only to show your rooms, item prices, quantities, and totals on this device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, tracking, or marketing.

## Sharing

Room Budget does not sell, rent, or share personal information. There is no server transmission of your budget. A copy leaves the app only if you copy it and then paste it somewhere yourself.

On-device storage is provided by iOS. That storage is not used by this app to send your budget to a third party.

## Tracking

Room Budget does not track you across apps or websites. It does not use Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt, because it does not request permission to track.

## Children’s privacy

Room Budget is a general-audience household tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app is not part of the Made for Kids program.

## Data retention and deletion

Your budget remains on the device until you delete it.

• Delete a room or a furniture item with the delete button in the app. Deleting a room also deletes the furniture stored in that room.
• Deleting Room Budget from your iPhone or iPad removes the data stored by the app on that device.

The developer cannot delete the on-device list for you, because the developer does not receive a copy of it.

## Security

Your list is kept in the app’s private storage on the device. Room Budget does not transmit that list over the internet. Protect access to your device with a passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. No method of storage is perfect, and this policy does not promise that a person with access to your unlocked device cannot read the list.

## International use

You can download Room Budget from the Apple App Store. The budget is handled on the device where you installed the app. The developer does not transfer your budget to another country, because the developer does not receive it.
