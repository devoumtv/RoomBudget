# Privacy Policy for Room Budget

**Effective date:** October 6, 2026  
**App name:** Room Budget  
**Package name / Bundle ID:** com.ldhkuqw.roombudgetfv0t  
**Stores:** Google Play and the Apple App Store

This policy describes how the Room Budget mobile app handles information. It applies to the Android app on Google Play and the iOS app on the Apple App Store. The same practices apply on both stores.

## Summary

Room Budget estimates furniture costs on your device. It does not require an account. It does not collect, transmit, or sell personal information. The lists you create stay on your phone or tablet.

## Information the app does not collect

Room Budget does not collect:

• Name, email address, phone number, or other account details
• Location
• Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
• Advertising identifiers
• Device identifiers for tracking
• Usage analytics, crash reports, or diagnostics sent to the developer
• Payment information

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or social-network services. It does not ask you to sign in.

## Information stored on your device

When you use the app, it saves what you type so it is still there the next time you open it:

• Room names and emoji
• Furniture names and emoji
• Prices and quantities

This information is stored locally on your device with the operating system’s app storage. It is not uploaded to the developer and it is not stored on a server controlled by the app. If you type a person’s name into a room or item name, that text remains on your device. The developer never receives it.

## Clipboard

The Total page has a Copy estimate button. When you tap it, Room Budget places a text copy of your rooms, items, and totals on the device clipboard. The app does not read other clipboard contents. It does not send the copied text to the developer or to any other service. What you do with the clipboard after that, such as pasting into another app, is controlled by you and by that other app.

## How information is used

The app uses the information you enter only to show your rooms, item prices, quantities, and totals on this device. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or marketing.

## Sharing

Room Budget does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. There is no server transmission of your budget. The only way a copy leaves the app is if you copy it and then paste or share it yourself.

The app is built with Flutter. On-device storage is provided by the operating system. Those components run on your device and are not used by this app to send your budget to a third party.

## Children’s privacy

Room Budget is a general-audience household tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information, there is no child profile to create or delete.

## Data retention and deletion

Your budget remains on the device until you delete it.

• Delete a room or a furniture item with the delete button in the app. Deleting a room also deletes the furniture stored in that room.
• Uninstalling Room Budget removes the data stored by the app on that device.

The developer cannot delete the on-device list for you, because the developer does not receive a copy of it.

## Security

Your list is kept in the app’s private storage on the device. Room Budget does not transmit that list over the internet. Protect access to your device with the lock method you choose. No method of storage is perfect, and this policy does not promise that a person with access to your unlocked device cannot read the list.

## International use

The app can be installed from Google Play or the Apple App Store. Processing of the budget happens on the device where you installed the app. The developer does not transfer your budget to another country, because the developer does not receive it.
