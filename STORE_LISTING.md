## Google Play

Room Budget

**Short description** (61 / 80 characters)

Estimate furniture costs for every room, then copy the total.

**Full description**

Room Budget helps you estimate furniture costs before you buy. The app opens empty. You add the rooms, you enter the prices, and you stay in control of every number.

🏠 Rooms
Create each space you want to furnish. Give it a name and an emoji, then edit or delete it whenever the plan changes.

🪑 Items
Add sofas, tables, lamps, and anything else. Set a price and a quantity for each piece. Every item stays attached to the room you choose.

💶 Total
See a room-by-room breakdown and one grand total in euros. Tap the copy button to place the whole estimate on your clipboard, ready to paste into notes or a message.

What you can do
• Start from a blank list, with no sample rooms or sample prices
• Add, edit, and delete rooms and furniture
• Enter quantities and see each line total
• Compare rooms with a simple share of the grand total
• Copy the full estimate with one button
• Use the app offline

Room Budget does not ask you to create an account. It does not show ads. Prices are the estimates you type, so the total always reflects your own plan.

Your rooms and prices stay on this device. Plan the living room, the bedroom, and every corner in between.
